Core Subsystem Architecture

Evolver Engine
Variant Iteration

Continuous self-improvement engine managing prompt/system mutations, ancestral lineage tracking, and promotion governance.

  • Zero Side-Effect Proposal: ProposalService creates candidate variants only with zero live runtime impact.
  • Approval Gate: ApplicationService strictly enforces approved=True and strictly higher mean score than baseline.
  • Immutable Audit Log: Writes to execution_audit before altering variant status in database.
SOUP Engine
Offline Probing

Small Offline Uniform Probe engine executing deterministic multi-variant comparisons against standardized evaluation sets.

  • Deterministic Scoring: Keyword-overlap & diversity scoring with stopword filtering bounded to [0, 100].
  • Strict Winner Selection: Only challengers that strictly beat baseline mean score are marked winners.
  • Decision Introspection: Generates NNsight-style DecisionTrace with win/loss/tie stats and human-readable explanations.

Safety & Promotion Pipeline

1

Variant Proposal (Candidate Status)

A new mutation is proposed through ProposalService.propose_variant(). It calculates generation from parent, serializes the mutation record, and saves to SQLite in candidate status with 0 live side-effects.

2

SOUP Offline Experiment Comparison

SoupService.run_comparison() executes all test cases across baseline and challenger variants, records every trial result to soup_trials, and computes statistical margin.

3

Decision Introspection & Evidence Generation

build_decision_trace_from_report() builds an explainable trace detailing case-by-case outcomes and compiles PromotionEvidence.

4

Gated Promotion & Audit Logging

ApplicationService.apply_variant() verifies human/system approval flag, validates evidence score delta, writes an append-only audit record to execution_audit, and promotes variant to active.
